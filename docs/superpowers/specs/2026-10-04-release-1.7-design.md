# Release 1.7.0: levels, shorter rounds, school lineature (design, 2026-10-04)

Audience: children ~4–6 (round, menu, album) and their parents (parent area).
Release target: 1.7.0 (base: `main` at `v1.6.1`).

## Goal
Give every exercise a clear, nameable ladder of four levels that parents can set directly and that the album mirrors; make rounds short enough for a small child's attention span; show letters the way school shows them (Lineatur 1 with the little house, Grundschrift); stop quantities from always looking like dice; put names on the menu tiles.

## Scope (items from the user)
| # | Item | Section |
|---|---|---|
| A | Parents set the level per exercise | 1 |
| B | Lettering on menu tiles: Mengenblitz, Zahlenblitz, Buchstabenblitz, Silbenblitz | 7 |
| 1 | Rounds half as long | 3 |
| 2 | Quantities not always in dice layout | 4 |
| 3 | Letters on Lineatur 1 with a "Dachhaus" at the left margin | 5 |
| 4 | A clearly readable font, e.g. Andika (SIL) | 6 |
| 5 | Predefined levels, default level 1, parents can pick a higher one per exercise | 1 |
| 6 | Stickers grouped by level and exercise | 2 |

A and 5 are the same feature and are designed together.

## Decisions (agreed with the user)
- D1 Fixed four-level ladders **replace** the per-exercise content settings.
- D2 A level set by parents is a **start point** with automatic advance (never down) plus a per-exercise **"Level festhalten"** switch; with fixed display duration the level is respected too.
- D3 Board and answer glyphs use the Grundschrift **Playwrite DE Grund**; Andika stays for all other text.
- D4 The existing 16 sticker pages are reassigned **4 × 4** to exercise × level.
- ⚠ D5 still open: look of Lineatur 1 (middle band, baseline, house colours). The defaults below follow the common school template; a reference from the user's school would override them.

## 1 · Levels (items A + 5)

### Today
Each exercise has a dynamic stage list derived from settings (`quantity.max/layout/addition`, `digits.range/addition`, `letters.case`, syllable pool). `adaptive.js` walks a single `complexity` index through it; Plus stages sit at the top. With fixed display duration `effectiveLevel()` ignores the stored level and uses `fixedComplexity` (top non-Plus stage). Quantity starts at "bis 5", the others at index 0. Parents cannot pick a stage.

### Ladders (D1)
Every exercise gets a fixed ladder of **four levels**. Each level has 1–4 internal **steps**; the adaptive logic works on steps inside a level exactly like today's `complexity`.

| Exercise | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mengen | bis 5 mit Muster — steps: 3, 4, 5 (structured) | bis 6, auch durcheinander — 5 mixed, 6 structured, 6 mixed | bis 10 — 8 structured, 8 mixed, 10 structured, 10 mixed | Plus — Summe bis 5, bis 10 |
| Zahlen | 0–5 | 0–10 — 0–9, 0–10 | 0–20 | Plus — Summe bis 5, bis 10 |
| Buchstaben | Großbuchstaben | Kleinbuchstaben | groß und klein gemischt | gemischt, 6 Antworten mit ähnlichen Buchstaben |
| Silben | Silben | Wörter aus zwei offenen Silben | alle Wörter | alle Wörter ohne Farbhilfe |

- Settings removed: `quantity.max`, `quantity.layout`, `quantity.addition`, `digits.range`, `digits.addition`, `letters.case`. Kept: known letters, own words, syllable colours (applies to levels 1–3), speech, sounds, timing.
- Availability: a level is playable when its content exists (syllables: words of that level from known letters; letters level 4: ≥ 4 known letters, uses up to 6). If the chosen level is not playable the round uses the highest playable level below it, and the parent area says why.
- Rejected: keeping the settings and only letting parents pick a start step from today's dynamic list ("Level 3" would change meaning with the settings; stickers could not be tied to levels).

### Behaviour (D2)
- New profiles: Level 1, step 1 everywhere (quantity no longer starts at "bis 5").
- Parent area, per exercise: level picker (segmented 1–4 with short description) and a switch **"Level festhalten"** (default off).
- Automatic display duration on:
  - Inside a level: today's rules (3 right → shorter, then next step; 2 of 3 wrong → longer, then previous step). Never drops below step 1 of the current level.
  - Top step + shortest duration + 3 right → level marked *mastered*. At the end of the round, if not held and a next playable level exists: level + 1, step 1, duration = start value. Round end shows "Level 2 geschafft!" and the new sticker page.
  - Automatic never goes down a level; only parents do that.
- Fixed display duration: no automation; the round uses the **last step** of the current level (same idea as today's `fixedComplexity`, now per level, so the parent's choice is respected).
- "Stufen zurücksetzen" → every exercise back to Level 1, step 1; hold switches stay.

### Data model
```
profile.levels[ex] = { level: 0..3, step, durationMs, streak, recent, mastered }
profile.settings.hold[ex] = boolean
```
Exercise contract change: `levels(settings)` returns `[{ label, steps: [...] , available }]` (4 entries); `maxComplexity/startComplexity/fixedComplexity/stages` are replaced by it. `createTask(level, …)` receives `{ level, step }`.

### Migration (schema 1 → 2)
`storage.migrate()` gets `upgradeV1toV2` (the placeholder already exists), run before `normalizeProfile` so the old settings are still readable. The old effective stage is mapped to level + nearest step:
- Mengen: Plus → L4; max ≤ 5 structured → L1 (3/4/5 → step 1/2/3); max ≤ 6 → L2; else L3.
- Zahlen: 0–5 → L1, 0–9/0–10 → L2, 0–20 → L3, Plus → L4.
- Buchstaben: upper → L1, lower → L2, mixed → L3.
- Silben: index → L1–L3.
- `rewards.unlockedPages` is dropped (derived, see 2). Stars and stickers stay untouched.
Exports from 1.7 are not importable in 1.6 (existing "newer version" message).

## 2 · Stickers by exercise and level (item 6)
The 16 existing pages map 4 × 4 (D4), themes chosen to fit the tile:

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mengen (apple basket) | Obst | Gemüse & Pilze | Leckereien | Essen |
| Zahlen (blocks) | Spielzeug | Fahrzeuge | Weltraum | Klötzchenwelt |
| Buchstaben (ABC blocks) | Tiere | Meer | Dinos | Quatschwesen |
| Silben (clapping hands) | Mein Zimmer | Küche | Kochen | Zauberei |

- A page is open when the child has reached that level in that exercise (or a parent set it). A good round gives a sticker from the page of the exercise + level just played; a full page gives bonus stars as today.
- Pages that already contain collected stickers stay visible even if their level is not reached yet (no child loses anything).
- Stars stay as score and bonus; they no longer open pages. `STARS_PER_PAGE` and `unlockedPagesFor` go away.
- Album: four groups (tile image as group header), four level tabs each; a locked page shows the lock plus "Level N" and the exercise image instead of a star count.
- Sticker ids (`page/name`) do not change.

## 3 · Shorter rounds (item 1)
- `ROUND_LENGTH` 10 → 5. Sticker from 4 of 5 (`STICKER_MIN_CORRECT` 4, keeps the 80 % bar).
- Round-end thresholds become ratios: great ≥ 80 % (4–5), good ≥ 50 % (3), else practised. Bubble and robot follow the same thresholds.
- `ALMOST` phrases: "Ab vier Richtigen …". The hint reads "3 von 5 – ab 4 gibt's einen Sticker" (already built from the constants).
- Adaptive streaks continue across rounds (they live in `profile.levels`), so shorter rounds do not slow down adaptation.
- Consequence: about twice as many stickers per minute played. With 8 stickers per page tied to a level, that matches the level pace well.

## 4 · Quantity layouts (item 2)
`structured` no longer means "dice for ≤ 6, ten-frame above". Each task picks one pattern from a pool, then a random quarter turn and/or mirror:

| Pattern | Counts | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Würfel | 1–6 | today's dice |
| Reihe / Fünferreihe | 1–10 | one row; 6–10 as 5 + rest in a second row ("Kraft der Fünf") |
| Paare | 2–10 | domino columns of two |
| Zwei Gruppen | 3–8 | two small clusters, e.g. 5 = 3 + 2 |
| Ring | 3–8 | evenly on a circle |

- The same pattern is not used twice in a row within a round.
- `random` (scattered) is unchanged; mixed steps alternate structured/random as today.
- Unit test: for every pattern, count and transform, all points lie in [0.1, 0.9] and keep `MIN_DIST`.

## 5 · Lineatur 1 with Dachhaus (item 3)
- New module `js/ui/lineatur.js`: `lineatur({ text, font })` builds an inline SVG (no static asset): four lines (Oberlinie, Mittellinie, Grundlinie, Unterlinie), shaded middle band, a small house at the left margin — roof in the upper band, house with door in the middle band, cellar in the lower band.
- The letter is an SVG `<text>` on the baseline in the same SVG, so it sits exactly on the Grundlinie; band heights come from the font metrics (x-height = middle band, ascender = top line, descender = bottom line). `dom.js` gets a small `svg()` helper (namespace-aware twin of `h()`).
- Default colours (⚠ D5): lines `#7FA3D9`, baseline `#E2574C` and slightly thicker, middle band `#FFF4C2`, roof `#E8705F`, house `#FFD66B`, cellar `#8C7A6B`; all as CSS tokens.
- Used for the letter on the board (flash and solution). Answer buttons stay plain glyphs in the same font (too small for lines).
- Parent switch "Buchstaben auf Schreiblinien zeigen" (default on). Syllables could use the same component later; not in 1.7.

Mockup (shared in the session, not committed): A = Andika with bands from its metrics, B = Grundschrift with bands from its metrics, C = Grundschrift on equal bands.

## 6 · Font (item 4)
Andika is already the app font since 1.0 (`css/app.css`, `assets/fonts/`, verified rendering). Two observations from the mockup:
- Andika's proportions (upper : middle : lower ≈ 0.56 : 1 : 0.5) do not fit Lineatur 1, whose bands are roughly equal; capitals end well below the top line.
- The 700 weight used on the board looks heavy and almost slanted in the "a".

Decision (D3): **Playwrite DE Grund** (Google Fonts, OFL 1.1, the German Grundschrift, ≈ 0.76 : 1 : 0.82) for board and answer glyphs (digits, letters, syllables), weight 400; Andika stays for all other UI text. +21 KB woff2 (`assets/fonts/playwrite-de-grund-latin-400-normal.woff2`, licence added to `OFL.txt`), precached.
Rejected: keeping Andika at 400 with bands from Andika's metrics (mockup A).

## 7 · Menu lettering (item B)
- Each tile gets its name as real text under the image: Mengenblitz, Zahlenblitz, Buchstabenblitz, Silbenblitz (`menuTitle` in each exercise module; `title` stays for the parent area). Text via `h()`, never innerHTML.
- Style: Andika 700, ink colour of the tile's edge darkened, white outline (`paint-order: stroke`), size `clamp()` so "Buchstabenblitz" fits a phone tile; image shrinks to make room.
- `aria-label` becomes the tile name.
- Note: the UX review aimed for "no text in the child area" for non-readers; the image stays dominant, the word is a bonus for parents and early readers.
